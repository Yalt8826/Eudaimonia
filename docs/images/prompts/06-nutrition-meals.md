# Nutrition detail

## Prompt

High-fidelity UI design mockup of a personal life-OS app called Eudaimonia, 'Matte & Torn' design language: a matte textured black background (#0B0B0C) with very subtle static grain, under which live light torn-paper sheets — sections appear where the black skin is torn open, each section on its own soft paper tint (mint, sky blue, blush, butter) with slightly ragged dark-fringed edges, flat (no blur) like real paper; UI buttons, nav bubbles and interactive chips are dark frosted glass (subtle white overlay, 1px translucent border, soft backdrop blur, rounded). Two-accent system: vivid teal (#2DD4BF) marks things the user acts on; deep plum purple (#B872F2) marks AI-agent elements. Fixed status colors: green #27963C on paper / #3FB950 on glass (alive/ok), red #F85149 (dead/error), amber #D29922 (paused/pending). Text on paper is warm dark ink (#2B2926) — print-like, high contrast. Clean IBM Plex-style sans-serif and serif on paper, small monospaced muted captions. Minimal, modern, premium productivity-app aesthetic, vector-crisp, high detail, professional Dribbble shot. Short legible labels only; generous whitespace; no clutter, no light theme, no lorem ipsum walls. Portrait smartphone app screen, tall 9:19.5 aspect ratio. Navigation: a small cluster of floating dark-glass bubble buttons at the bottom-right corner (icon-only, active bubble ringed teal) instead of any fixed bottom bar; no bubbles at all on the wake screen. SCREEN: a nutrition log. Top: a muted mono coverage line '0 of last 7 · dormant since Aug 16' with a neutral muted dot (not red), and beneath it a muted mono reference line 'target 2300–2500 kcal · 150 g P' so values render against their benchmark. Below, glass day cards, each with 2–3 meal rows (item, quantity) and right-aligned mono numbers like '~620 kcal · ~34 g P' — tildes on kcal AND protein alike (both are agent estimates) — protein values in bold, kcal values in regular-weight muted mono (data differentiates by weight, never by accent hue). One card has a folded row '+3 more'. A small teal button 'log a meal'. Honest, quiet data — no alarm colors.

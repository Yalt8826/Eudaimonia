# Plaza — desktop wide

## Prompt

High-fidelity UI design mockup of a personal life-OS app called Eudaimonia, 'Matte & Torn' design language: a matte textured black background (#0B0B0C) with very subtle static grain, under which live light torn-paper sheets — sections appear where the black skin is torn open, each section on its own soft paper tint (mint, sky blue, blush, butter) with slightly ragged dark-fringed edges, flat (no blur) like real paper; UI buttons, nav bubbles and interactive chips are dark frosted glass (subtle white overlay, 1px translucent border, soft backdrop blur, rounded). Two-accent system: vivid teal (#2DD4BF) marks things the user acts on; deep plum purple (#B872F2) marks AI-agent elements. Fixed status colors: green #27963C on paper / #3FB950 on glass (alive/ok), red #F85149 (dead/error), amber #D29922 (paused/pending). Text on paper is warm dark ink (#2B2926) — print-like, high contrast. Clean IBM Plex-style sans-serif and serif on paper, small monospaced muted captions. Minimal, modern, premium productivity-app aesthetic, vector-crisp, high detail, professional Dribbble shot. Short legible labels only; generous whitespace; no clutter, no light theme, no lorem ipsum walls. Wide 16:9 desktop app window, dark glassmorphism dashboard. Header bar: 'Eudaimonia' wordmark in plain off-white (never plum — plum is reserved for AI-agent content), mono date center, settings and a teal '+ capture' button right. Category icons stay muted gray. Below, a three-column grid of frosted glass cards: TODAY'S PLAN (teal left rule), WAITING ON with amber badge 3 and inline done/roll/drop text buttons, AGENTS with status dots, WAKE chip '4 of 7', NUTRITION dormant line, YESTERDAY with a plum-attributed line, SERVER with green dots, and a bottom strip READING '80 queued · digest paused'. A tiny muted keyboard-hint line: 'c capture · g i inbox · g w week'. Same calm two-accent system, airy, information-dense but organized.

# Wake screen (hard-mode)

## Prompt

Extreme-minimal full-screen mobile UI. The ENTIRE screen is one vibrant teal (#2DD4BF) surface — full bleed, no glass, no navigation, no header, no other chrome. Centered dark ink (#0B0B0C) text: 'GOOD MORNING' and beneath it a large thin clock reading '07:14 · tap time'. Below center, one enormous rounded full-width button in dark ink with teal text reading 'I'M AWAKE'. Near the bottom, one small muted dark line: 'woke earlier? log ~recall instead'. Nothing else — a 5-second half-asleep tap target. High contrast ink-on-teal, calm and bright.

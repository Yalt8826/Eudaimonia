# Weekly review — paper tier

## Prompt

A beautifully typeset reading surface: a torn-open section of the matte textured black skin revealing a cream paper sheet (flat, no blur, ragged dark-fringed edge), elegant serif typography in warm dark ink, narrow 65ch measure column, generous line height. Top: a neutral rounded callout box titled 'Coverage' with small mono lines and tiny green/amber/muted status dots. Below, an italic short paragraph introduced by a thin plum left rule (AI-narrated section). Then document sections with serif headings: 'Plan vs actual' with a small table, 'Habits' with rolling-window lines like 'wake within ±30 min: 4 of 7', 'Wellness' with '~1589 kcal/day est.' style figures, 'Carry-forward' as an empty-checkbox task list. No cards, no dashboard chrome — a quiet print essay on torn paper. Portrait phone aspect.

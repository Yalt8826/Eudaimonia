# Plaza — Today view (home)

## Prompt

High-fidelity UI design mockup of a personal life-OS app called Eudaimonia, 'Matte & Torn' design language: a matte textured black background (#0B0B0C) with very subtle static grain, under which live light torn-paper sheets — sections appear where the black skin is torn open, each section on its own soft paper tint (mint, sky blue, blush, butter) with slightly ragged dark-fringed edges, flat (no blur) like real paper; UI buttons, nav bubbles and interactive chips are dark frosted glass (subtle white overlay, 1px translucent border, soft backdrop blur, rounded). Two-accent system: vivid teal (#2DD4BF) marks things the user acts on; deep plum purple (#B872F2) marks AI-agent elements. Fixed status colors: green #27963C on paper / #3FB950 on glass (alive/ok), red #F85149 (dead/error), amber #D29922 (paused/pending). Text on paper is warm dark ink (#2B2926) — print-like, high contrast. Clean IBM Plex-style sans-serif and serif on paper, small monospaced muted captions. Minimal, modern, premium productivity-app aesthetic, vector-crisp, high detail, professional Dribbble shot. Short legible labels only; generous whitespace; no clutter, no light theme, no lorem ipsum walls. Portrait smartphone app screen, tall 9:19.5 aspect ratio. Navigation: a small cluster of floating dark-glass bubble buttons at the bottom-right corner (icon-only, active bubble ringed teal) instead of any fixed bottom bar; no bubbles at all on the wake screen. SCREEN: the home screen, the Plaza. Top: small muted mono date line 'Today · Wed Oct 8' with a settings gear. A frosted glass card with a teal left border titled TODAY'S PLAN listing three schedule lines. Below, a glass card WAITING ON with a small amber count badge showing 3, a neutral gray icon (NOT plum — these are human commitments, plum is reserved for AI-agent content), and two list rows. A habit row: a teal pill chip 'WAKE ● 4 of 7' beside a tappable teal-outlined chip 'tap when you wake'. Two muted mono coverage lines: 'NUTRITION · 0 of 7 · last logged Aug 16' with a small muted gray dot (behavioral dormancy — never red, never amber) and 'READING · 80 queued · digest paused' with a tiny amber dot (paused is the only amber use here). A YESTERDAY section: one muted line, then a line marked with a plum dot 'narrated by Noesis'. A bottom row 'AGENTS' with one red and one amber status dot. A round teal floating action button with a plus, bottom right. Five-slot glass bottom navigation bar.
